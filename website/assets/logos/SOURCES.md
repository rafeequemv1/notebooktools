# Logo sources

Brand marks used to show which apps NotebookTools imports from. The marks
belong to their owners; they are shown only to identify those products
(nominative use) and imply no endorsement.

- chatgpt (OpenAI mark), claude, gemini, perplexity, grok, kimi:
  LobeHub Icons, `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.102.0 (MIT) — https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
- youtube, x, reddit, googledocs: Simple Icons 16.36.0 (CC0 1.0) — https://simpleicons.org
