## Export NotebookLM chat to PDF, Markdown and more

The **Export** button in the NotebookTools ribbon saves your NotebookLM work three ways: the **last answer** as a print-ready PDF, a Word file or Markdown, or copied with its citation numbers and a list of their sources (**Copy with citations**); the **whole chat** as Markdown, plain text, JSON, CSV, HTML or PDF; or the **whole notebook**, every source's text and every note, as a ZIP of files. Files are named after your notebook.

### How to use it

1. Open a notebook and its chat in NotebookLM.
2. Click **Export** in the NotebookTools ribbon at the top of the notebook.
3. Under **Last answer only**, choose PDF, Word or Markdown; under **Whole chat**, Markdown, Plain text, JSON, CSV, HTML or PDF; under **Whole notebook**, **Sources and notes (ZIP)**.
4. For PDF, a print-ready page opens: choose **Save as PDF** in Chrome's print dialog. Other formats download straight away.

### Why it helps

A good NotebookLM session is a research trail: the questions you asked and the cited answers you got. Exporting keeps a copy you can share, file with a project or paste into a report. Markdown suits note apps, CSV and JSON suit spreadsheets and scripts, and PDF suits handouts and records. The single-answer export keeps headings, lists, tables and bold text, which helps when the answer is a worksheet, exam paper or lesson plan you want to print. Citation chips and buttons are left out, so the file reads like a normal document.

## Chat export: frequently asked questions

### Can I copy a NotebookLM answer with its citations?

Yes. In the ribbon, choose Export → Copy with citations. The answer is copied with its citation numbers in place and a numbered list of the sources they point to, ready to paste into Google Docs, Word or Notion.

### Can you export a NotebookLM chat to PDF?

Yes, with NotebookTools. Click Export in the ribbon, choose PDF under Whole chat, and a print-ready page opens; pick Save as PDF in Chrome's print dialog.

### Can I export a single NotebookLM answer?

Yes. The answer export saves the last answer with headings, lists, tables and bold text kept, as a PDF, a Word file or Markdown.
