## Edit NotebookLM slide decks: real text, real PowerPoint

NotebookLM's slide decks are pictures: every slide is one flat image, so you can't fix a typo or a date. The NotebookTools slide editor reads each slide on your computer and rebuilds it with real text boxes, keeping the look of the original. Pictures, charts and icons are cut out as separate pictures, and the background behind removed text is filled in. Diagrams get the same care: labels in small pills and boxes become editable text, and a diagram whose drawings are joined by arrows is split into one picture per drawing. The online editor also comes in [Spanish](https://www.notebooktools.com/es/editar-diapositivas-notebooklm), [Portuguese](https://www.notebooktools.com/pt/editar-slides-notebooklm) and [Traditional Chinese](https://www.notebooktools.com/zh-tw/edit-notebooklm-slides). Then download PowerPoint, a PDF with selectable text, or images.

### How to use it

1. In NotebookLM, open the slide deck in Studio and click **Edit** next to it. Without the extension, download the deck as a PDF and open it in the [online slide editor](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/edit-notebooklm-slides).
2. Wait a few seconds per slide while it reads the text. Click **Original** any time to compare with the slide as NotebookLM made it.
3. Click any text to edit it: words, font, size, bold, italic, colour, even single words inside a line. Drag pictures, split a picture into its parts or merge two, and add text boxes, shapes and speaker notes.
4. Click **Download PowerPoint**, or use the menu beside it for a PDF with real text, PNG images or every picture as a ZIP.
5. Your edits are saved in your browser. Reopen any deck from the **Presentations** tab in the notebooks dashboard.

## Slide editor: frequently asked questions

### Can I edit the text on NotebookLM slides?

Not in NotebookLM itself, because each slide is an image. The NotebookTools slide editor reads the text and turns it into real text boxes you can edit, then saves PowerPoint or PDF.

### Will it look like the original?

Close to it. Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, colours, superscripts and number badges are matched, and pictures keep their place. Click **Original** to compare, and fix anything by hand.

### Are my slides uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading the slides, editing and exporting all happen in your browser.
