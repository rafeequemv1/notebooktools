A NotebookLM Audio Overview player and Studio files in one place

NotebookLM keeps each Audio Overview inside the notebook that made it, so catching up on episodes means opening notebooks one by one. The NotebookTools Podcast view finds Audio Overviews across all your notebooks and plays them in one player that remembers where you stopped. Each notebook's Studio tab lists its Studio files and can generate new ones.

How to use it

  1. Open a notebook in the NotebookTools hub and go to its Studio tab to see its Studio files.
  2. To make something new, choose an output type such as audio, quiz, flashcards, report or slides.
  3. Pick a format and length, and add optional instructions.
  4. Generate it. The output appears in the list and opens directly in NotebookLM.
  5. Open the Podcast tab to scan every notebook for Audio Overviews and play them in one player.

Why it helps

Audio Overviews are made for listening on a walk or a commute, but finding last week's episode in one of 50 notebooks is slow. One player with every episode, and a saved position in each, turns them into a podcast feed for your own material. The Studio tab gives you the same overview for quizzes, reports and slide decks.

Studio & podcasts: frequently asked questions

Where are NotebookLM Audio Overviews saved?

Each Audio Overview is stored in the Studio panel of the notebook that created it. NotebookTools collects them from every notebook into one player.

Does the player remember where I stopped?

Yes. It saves your position in each Audio Overview and resumes from there the next time you press play.

Is the Audio Overview player free?

The player and Studio files are part of NotebookTools Pro, a one-time $49 payment. Creating Audio Overviews in NotebookLM itself is free within Google's usage limits.
