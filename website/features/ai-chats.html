Send ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and other AI chats to NotebookLM

NotebookTools adds a Send to NotebookLM button to AI chats and social posts. One click saves the whole conversation, with who said what, to a NotebookLM notebook as a clean text source. It works on ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity (with its list of sources), Grok and Kimi, and on Reddit threads, LinkedIn posts and articles, X posts and Google Docs.

How to use it

  1. Open a conversation in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok or Kimi. Shared chat links work too.
  2. Click Send to NotebookLM: in the chat's top bar on ChatGPT and Gemini, at the top right on the others.
  3. Check the title and the number of messages in the panel.
  4. Pick a notebook, or type a name to create a new one.
  5. Click Send to notebook. The chat is saved as a source titled with its app, for example [CHATGPT] ….
  6. On a Reddit thread you get the post and its first 50 top-level comments; on LinkedIn the post or article; in Google Docs, Copy text to NotebookLM saves a text copy of the document.

Why it helps

Good answers end up scattered across several AI apps, and none of them can be searched together. Saved in a notebook, the conversation sits next to your papers, videos and notes, where NotebookLM can cite it, compare it with other sources and turn it into a report or an Audio Overview.

Only what is loaded on the page is saved, so scroll through very long chats or threads first. The button follows the site's light or dark theme and stays out of the way on pages without a conversation.

AI chats: frequently asked questions

Can I add a ChatGPT conversation to NotebookLM?

Yes. Open the chat, click Send to NotebookLM at the top, pick a notebook and click Send to notebook. The conversation is saved as one text source, with your questions and ChatGPT's answers labelled.

Which sites does it work on?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok and Kimi, plus Reddit, LinkedIn, X and Google Docs. YouTube videos have their own Send to NotebookLM button under the player.

Does a Google Doc stay in sync?

No. Copy text to NotebookLM saves a text copy. For a source that stays in sync, add the document from Google Drive in NotebookLM itself.
