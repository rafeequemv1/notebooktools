## Turn a NotebookLM slide deck into a LinkedIn or Instagram carousel

The carousel maker turns a slide deck from NotebookLM's Studio into a post-ready carousel: a LinkedIn document carousel as one PDF, or Instagram images as a ZIP of JPGs, at 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080. Your handle, page numbers and a swipe hint go on every page, and the first page can show the deck's title.

### How to use it

1. Open a notebook that has a slide deck in Studio.
2. Click **Create** in the NotebookTools ribbon and choose **Carousel**.
3. Pick the deck, then choose **LinkedIn PDF** or **Instagram images** and the size.
4. Type your handle, choose the background (match each slide, light or dark) and whether to show page numbers.
5. Click **Download**.

### Why it helps

Slide decks are already made of short, visual points, which is exactly what performs as a carousel. Re-making them in a design tool takes an hour; the carousel maker does it in a few seconds and keeps the slides sharp.
