Why this launch matters

Your Studio deck framed the launch as a focused push into mid-market teams that already research with NotebookLM. The blog keeps that narrative and turns each slide into a scannable section.

Ideal customer profile

Operators who manage research libraries, share briefs with stakeholders, and need exports that travel outside the notebook. Position the product as the bridge from Studio artifacts to publishable guides.

Channel mix

Lead with product-led content (this blog), amplify with short clips from the deck, and route readers into a NotebookLM notebook that holds the source slides.

What to do next

Export Markdown or HTML, drop the images into your CMS, and keep the Studio deck linked so updates stay one refresh away.
