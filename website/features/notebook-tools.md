## NotebookTools tools inside every NotebookLM notebook

Open a notebook and NotebookTools adds one slim ribbon above Sources, chat and Studio, in NotebookLM's own style. It groups the tools you reach for while you work: manage and filter sources, manage Studio files, copy or export the chat, insert and save prompts, create from the notebook, and file the notebook in a folder or under a tag without going back to your list.

### How to use it

1. **Sources:** **Manage** opens a dialog to search, filter, find duplicates, refresh and delete sources. **Type** shows only PDFs, web pages, videos or another kind in NotebookLM's Sources panel.
2. **Studio:** **Manage** lists the notebook's Studio files to search, open, download or delete; **Type** shows one kind of file; **Create** makes a blog post, a carousel or a snapshot document, or opens a slide deck in the slide editor.
3. **Chat:** **Copy** puts the whole chat on your clipboard as Markdown; **Queue** runs several prompts one after another, each when the last answer is done; **Prompts** inserts one of your prompts, saves what's in the chat box as a new one, or opens your prompts; **Export** saves the last answer, the chat or the whole notebook, or copies the last answer with its citations and a list of their sources. **Flashcards** (in the Studio group) sends the notebook's flashcards and quizzes to Anki, Quizlet, Kahoot, Wayground or Moodle.
4. **Organize:** **Add to folder**, **Tags** and the star file the notebook in the dashboard as you work. **All notebooks** opens the dashboard.
5. Slide decks in Studio also get an **Edit** button that opens them in the slide editor.

### Why it helps

The tools sit where the work happens. You don't switch to another page to tidy sources, find a Studio file, keep a good prompt or file the notebook away, and the ribbon follows NotebookLM's light and dark themes.

On a narrow window the ribbon shortens its labels and then shows icons only, so it never pushes NotebookLM's panels around.

## In-notebook tools: frequently asked questions

### Where does the NotebookTools ribbon appear?

In every NotebookLM notebook, right above the Sources, chat and Studio panels. It is not shown on NotebookLM's notebook list.

### Can I hide it?

You can make it compact: the arrow at its end hides the labels and keeps the icons.

### Does it change my notebook?

Only when you ask: deleting, refreshing or renaming sources, deleting Studio files. Folders, tags and favorites are saved by NotebookTools in your browser.
