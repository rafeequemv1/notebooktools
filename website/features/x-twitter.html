Import X (Twitter) to NotebookLM as clean text

NotebookTools reads the X post, thread or X Article you have open and saves it to a NotebookLM notebook as one clean text source. A NotebookLM button appears in the column header on X, and the panel shows the thread title and how many posts it found before you import.

How to use it

  1. Open a post or thread on X.
  2. Click the NotebookLM button in the column header.
  3. Check the thread title and the number of posts in the panel.
  4. Pick a notebook, or name a new one.
  5. Click Import. The thread is saved as a text source.

Why it helps

Threads are where many researchers, founders and engineers post their best explanations, but they are hard to quote and easy to lose. Pasting an X link into NotebookLM may bring in little useful text, because X loads posts with scripts and often asks you to sign in. Saved as text, a thread can be searched, cited and combined with your papers and videos in the same notebook. You can then ask NotebookLM to summarize the argument, pull out the claims, or compare the thread with other sources on the same topic.

X / Twitter: frequently asked questions

Can I add a Twitter thread to NotebookLM?

Yes, with NotebookTools. Open the thread on X, click the NotebookLM button, pick a notebook and click Import. The thread is saved as one text source.

Does it work with X Articles?

Yes. Long-form X Articles import the same way as posts and threads.

Is the X (Twitter) to NotebookLM import free?

Yes. X import is free, like every other import option in NotebookTools.
