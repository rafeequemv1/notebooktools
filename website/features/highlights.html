Harvard Business Review

Why shared sources beat smart summaries

By Priya Raman · 8 min read

Most teams now ask an AI to summarise the reading. It is fast, and it feels thorough.

Teams that review sources together make better decisions than teams that rely on summaries alone. In our study of 140 product teams, groups that read the underlying documents caught twice as many risks.

The fix is not to abandon summaries, but to keep the sources one click away — and to save the passages that matter.

Save highlighted text to NotebookLM from any page

Select a passage on any web page, right-click and choose Add to NotebookLM. NotebookTools opens its popup with your selection, you pick a notebook, and the text is saved as a source titled with the page name.

How to use it

  1. Highlight the passage you want to keep on any page.
  2. Right-click and choose Add to NotebookLM. The NotebookTools popup opens with your selection.
  3. Pick the notebook the quote should go to.
  4. Click Add text to notebook. It is saved as a text source titled with the page name.

Why it helps

Adding a whole page brings in menus, comments and sidebars along with the paragraph you cared about. A highlight keeps only the quote, so NotebookLM's answers and citations point at exactly that text. Because the text comes from the page you already have open, it also works on pages NotebookLM can't fetch from a link, such as ones you are signed in to. Collect quotes from several articles into one notebook, then ask NotebookLM to compare them, build a reading summary, or turn them into flashcards for review.

Highlights: frequently asked questions

How do I add selected text to NotebookLM?

Highlight the text, right-click and choose Add to NotebookLM. Pick a notebook in the popup and click Add text to notebook.

Does each highlight count as a source?

Yes. Each import is one text source, so it uses one of the notebook's source slots: 50 on a free Google account.

Is saving highlights to NotebookLM free?

Yes. Highlights, webpages, YouTube and X imports are all free in NotebookTools.
