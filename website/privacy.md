# Privacy Policy

> NotebookTools privacy policy: no data sales. Imports use your NotebookLM session in Chrome; optional accounts store only your email, plan and purchase record.
> Source: https://www.notebooktools.com/privacy

Last updated: October 2026 · NotebookTools extension version 0.6.0

NotebookTools is an independent Chrome extension that helps you import content into your Google NotebookLM account — including YouTube videos, X (Twitter) posts and articles, webpage URLs, and text you highlight on the web. This policy explains what data the extension handles and what we do not collect.

## Summary

- Your NotebookLM content never passes through NotebookTools servers.
- An account is optional. If you create one, we store only your email, your plan (Free or Pro) and your purchase record.
- No sale of your personal data.
- Imports happen only when **you** click import or use the right-click menu.
- Data is sent from your browser to NotebookLM using your existing Google sign-in.

## What we collect

NotebookTools does not collect analytics. Imports go straight from your browser to NotebookLM; our servers never see the content you import.

- The extension reads page content **only when you choose to import** (for example, a YouTube URL, X post text, webpage URL, or text you highlighted).
- The extension stores lightweight preferences locally in Chrome on your device (for example, whether the welcome page was shown and your last-selected notebook).
- Your notebook list is fetched from NotebookLM using your existing signed-in Google session in the browser.

## Accounts and payments

Signing in is optional; every Free feature works without an account. If you create an account in the side panel, we store:

- Your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
- Your plan (Free or Pro) and when Pro started.
- For Pro purchases: the payment ID, amount, currency and the email used at checkout, so Pro can be matched to your account.

This data is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) in the EU (Ireland). Payments are processed by [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/privacy-policy), our merchant of record; we never see or store your card details. The extension keeps your sign-in session locally in Chrome on your device. To delete your account and its data, contact us via the [support page](https://www.notebooktools.com/support).

## What we send

When you click import, the extension sends the relevant source data to NotebookLM through Google's NotebookLM web APIs, using the same browser session you already use at [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com/). After import, Google’s NotebookLM terms apply to that notebook. See [NotebookLM privacy: does Google train on your data?](https://www.notebooktools.com/blog/notebooklm-privacy). Depending on what you import, this may include:

- YouTube video URLs (from watch pages you are viewing)
- Webpage URLs (from the tab you chose to import)
- Text extracted from X posts or articles (only when you click import on that post)
- Text you selected on a webpage (via right-click → Add to NotebookLM)

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data, and we share it only with the providers that run accounts and payments (Supabase and Dodo Payments).
- We do not use your data for advertising or unrelated purposes.
- We do not receive your Google password.
- We do not upload content unless you explicitly trigger an import.
- We do not log your browsing history.
- We do not track clicks, scroll position, or keystrokes.
- We do not use remote code — all extension code is included in the package you install from the Chrome Web Store.

## Chrome Web Store disclosures

For transparency on the Chrome Web Store, NotebookTools may process **website content** only when you initiate an import (URLs, selected text, or visible post/article text on X). If you create an optional account, we store your email address (personally identifiable information) and sign-in credentials (authentication information) to provide your plan; purchases are handled by Dodo Payments. We do not collect health, financial, communication, location, web history, or general user-activity data on NotebookTools servers.

## Third-party services

YouTube, X (Twitter), and NotebookLM are operated by their respective providers. Accounts are hosted by Supabase and payments are processed by Dodo Payments. Your use of those services is subject to their terms and privacy policies. NotebookTools is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, YouTube, X, or NotebookLM.

## Children

NotebookTools is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
