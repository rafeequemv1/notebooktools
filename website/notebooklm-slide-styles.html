Smart editorial deck

Business media

Asymmetrical cover, yellow accents, magazine-style headlines.

Style prompt
Build a slide deck from my notebook sources in a sharp digital business magazine style. Slide text must be plain text only with no markdown symbols. Cover slide: asymmetrical Swiss-style layout, a 2-5 word headline as the visual anchor, and a short subtitle that states the reader's problem. Colors: white or cool gray background, near-black text, electric yellow or red accents. Headlines should fill 30-50% of each slide with a 10:1 size jump over body copy. One message per slide. Use cutout-style photos and yellow marker highlights on key numbers. Ground every point in my sources.

Questions

How do I change the style of a NotebookLM slide deck?

Open Studio in your notebook, choose Slide deck, then the customise (pencil) option, and paste a style prompt from this page into the description box before you generate. With the NotebookTools extension you can also type / in the chat and pick a style.

Do these prompts change what the slides say?

No. Each prompt describes the look (colours, type, layout) and tells Gemini Notebook to take every fact from your own sources, so the content still comes from your notebook.

Can I use these slide styles for free?

Yes. Slide decks are part of NotebookLM's free plan, within its usage limits, and these prompts are free to copy.

Can I turn the finished deck into a blog post or carousel?

Yes. NotebookTools can turn a finished deck into a blog post with every slide as an image, or into a LinkedIn or Instagram carousel.
