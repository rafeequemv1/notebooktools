Is NotebookLM Free? Yes. Here’s What You Get (2026)

Updated November 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

Yes. NotebookLM, now called Gemini Notebook, is free with any personal Google account. The free plan gives you up to 100 notebooks with 50 sources each, cited answers from your own material, and Studio outputs such as Audio Overviews, slide decks, flashcards and quizzes. Paid Google AI plans raise the limits; you never need one to start.

Since September 2026, Google measures chat and Studio use with limits that refresh every few hours, up to a weekly cap, instead of a fixed number per day. Paid plans get more. Google changes these numbers and prices often, so check the plan page inside Gemini Notebook before you pay.

What the free plan includes

  • Notebooks and sources. Up to 100 notebooks, each with up to 50 sources: PDFs, Google Docs and Slides, web pages, YouTube videos, audio files and pasted text.
  • Cited chat. Ask questions across everything in a notebook. Every answer links back to the passage it came from, which is the main reason people use it.
  • Studio. Audio Overviews (podcast-style recaps), Video Overviews, slide decks, mind maps, reports and study guides, flashcards and quizzes, all from your own sources.
  • Sharing. Share a notebook with a study group or team so everyone works from the same sources.

For the full rules on sizes and counts, see our NotebookLM source limits guide.

When a paid plan is worth it

  • You regularly need more than 50 sources in one notebook, such as a large literature review or a whole course in one place.
  • You hit the usage limit for chat or Audio Overviews most weeks.
  • You already pay for Google AI Pro for Gemini or storage, in which case the higher NotebookLM limits come included.

Students, teachers and most solo researchers stay comfortable on the free plan. For a side-by-side of every tier, read NotebookLM pricing plans.

How to get more from the free plan

  1. One notebook per topic. Splitting a big project into a few focused notebooks keeps each under 50 sources and gives sharper answers.
  2. Remove duplicates and stale sources. The same PDF added twice uses two slots.
  3. Combine small sources. Ten short articles pasted into one Google Doc count as one source.
  4. Import in bulk, not one by one. Saves the time, if not the slots.

The free NotebookTools extension helps with each of these. It shows a source meter on every notebook, finds duplicate sources and imports links, open tabs and YouTube playlists in one run. Its notebooks table also keeps 100 notebooks in order with folders and tags.

How it compares to paid AI tools

ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro and Perplexity Pro each cost about $20 a month and are general assistants. NotebookLM is different: it answers only from the material you give it, with a citation for every claim, and the core of that is free. Many people pay for one general assistant and use NotebookLM free beside it. See NotebookLM vs ChatGPT and NotebookLM vs Gemini.

How to start for free

  1. Go to notebooklm.google.com and sign in with a Google account. There is no card and no trial.
  2. Create a notebook and add a PDF, a link or a YouTube video.
  3. Ask a question, or open Studio and make an Audio Overview.

FAQ

Is NotebookLM free?

Yes. NotebookLM, now called Gemini Notebook, is free with any personal Google account. The free plan includes up to 100 notebooks with 50 sources each, cited chat answers and Studio outputs such as Audio Overviews, slide decks, flashcards and quizzes, within usage limits.

How many sources can I add on the free plan?

Up to 50 sources per notebook and up to 100 notebooks. Each source can be up to 500,000 words or 200 MB, on every plan.

Are Audio Overviews free?

Yes. Audio Overviews and the other Studio outputs are included on the free plan, within usage limits that refresh over time. Paid plans raise those limits.

What do paid Google AI plans add to NotebookLM?

Higher limits. Google AI Pro, for example, raises you to 500 notebooks and 300 sources per notebook, with more chat and Studio usage. Google AI Plus and Ultra sit below and above Pro. Check Google's plan page for current prices in your country.

Is NotebookLM the same as Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in 2026. It is the same product, and existing notebooks carried over.

Is NotebookTools free?

Yes. NotebookTools is a free Chrome extension for NotebookLM, and every feature works on the free plan. Pro is an optional one-time $49 lifetime upgrade.
