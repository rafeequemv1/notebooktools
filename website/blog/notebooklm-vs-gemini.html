NotebookLM vs Gemini (and Gemini Notebook): which should you use?

Updated November 8, 2026 · 10 min read

Short answer

Gemini Notebook and NotebookLM are the same product. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in 2026; your notebooks, sources and the notebooklm.google.com address carried over. The real comparison is Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) vs the Gemini app: Gemini Notebook answers only from the sources you add, with a citation for every claim. The Gemini app is a general assistant that draws on its training and the web. Use the notebook for document research and studying, the app for everything else.

NotebookLM vs Gemini Notebook: what changed with the rename

Many people search "NotebookLM vs Gemini Notebook" expecting two tools. There is only one. In 2026 Google brought NotebookLM under the Gemini brand as Gemini Notebook:

  • Same product, new name. Notebooks, sources, chat history and Studio outputs carried over. Nothing to migrate.
  • Same limits. 100 notebooks and 50 sources per notebook on the free plan; 300 sources on Google AI Pro. See source limits.
  • Same price. Free with a Google account; paid limits come with Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra. See is NotebookLM free.
  • Separate from the Gemini app. Gemini Notebook is still its own workspace built around your sources. It is not a mode inside a normal Gemini chat.

In this guide "NotebookLM" and "Gemini Notebook" mean the same thing, and "Gemini" means the general Gemini app at gemini.google.com.

What makes Gemini Notebook different from a general-purpose AI assistant?

Gemini Notebook is built around a simple contract: you bring the sources, it answers from them. Upload lecture slides, court filings, product specs or a stack of articles, then ask questions knowing the responses are scoped to that material. It does not fill gaps with facts from outside your library, which cuts the risk of made-up answers when accuracy matters.

The Gemini app is Google's general conversational AI. It drafts emails, explains concepts, writes code and analyzes images without you loading documents first. When you need breadth and speed, the Gemini app wins. When you need depth tied to a defined evidence base, Gemini Notebook wins.

Sources and workflows

NotebookLM's strength is persistent source management. Once you import a PDF or YouTube video, it stays in your notebook until you remove it. You can query across all sources at once, generate study guides, and produce Audio Overviews that synthesize the full collection. Tools like NotebookTools make filling a notebook faster by importing from your browser in one click.

The Gemini app does not keep a source library in the same way. You can upload files to a conversation or use web search for current events, but context does not persist as an organized research project. For ongoing work (a thesis, a case, a product launch) the notebook model is more natural.

Citations and trust

For many professionals, citations decide it. NotebookLM shows which source and passage support each claim; click a citation and you jump to the text. That traceability is essential for academic work, legal research, journalism and anywhere you must verify claims.

The Gemini app can cite web pages when search is on, but it does not offer the same passage-level citations to your own documents. If a colleague asks "where did this number come from?", NotebookLM gives a direct answer.

Audio Overviews and unique features

NotebookLM's Audio Overview turns your sources into a podcast-style conversation, popular with students and commuters. Its Studio also makes Video Overviews, slide decks, mind maps, flashcards and quizzes from your notebook. The Gemini app offers voice chat but not an equivalent "summarize my whole source library as audio" workflow.

The Gemini app counters with broader capabilities: image generation and understanding, code execution, Deep Research across the web, and integration with Gmail and Docs. The two overlap only partly.

Pricing in 2026

Gemini Notebook is free with a Google account: 100 notebooks and 50 sources each, within usage limits. The Gemini app also has a free tier. One subscription upgrades both: Google AI Pro ($19.99/month in the US) unlocks Gemini's top models and raises NotebookLM to 500 notebooks and 300 sources per notebook. Google AI Plus and Ultra sit below and above it. Details in NotebookLM pricing plans.

Verdict: NotebookLM vs Gemini

Use Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) when your work depends on specific documents: course materials, client files, literature reviews, or any project where you need grounded answers with citations. Respect the source limits and treat each notebook as a focused workspace.

Use the Gemini app when you need a flexible assistant for tasks that do not revolve around a fixed document set: drafting, coding, quick explanations, or exploring topics you have not collected sources for yet.

Use both together: the Gemini app to brainstorm and draft, Gemini Notebook to check facts against your sources and produce cited summaries.

FAQ

Is Gemini Notebook the same as NotebookLM?

Yes. Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in 2026. It is the same product with the same features and limits, and existing notebooks carried over.

What is the difference between Gemini and NotebookLM?

NotebookLM (Gemini Notebook) answers only from the sources you add and cites the exact passage for each claim. The Gemini app is a general assistant that uses its training and the web, with no persistent source library.

Which is better for studying, Gemini or NotebookLM?

NotebookLM. Load your lectures, slides and readings, then generate study guides, flashcards, quizzes and Audio Overviews that stay tied to your course material. See how to use NotebookLM to study.

Does NotebookLM use Gemini?

Yes. NotebookLM runs on Google's Gemini models, wrapped in a source-grounded interface with its own features and limits.

Do I need to pay for both?

No. Both have free tiers. A single Google AI Pro subscription raises the limits in both the Gemini app and Gemini Notebook.
