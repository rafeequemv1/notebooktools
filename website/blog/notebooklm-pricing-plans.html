NotebookLM pricing plans explained (2026)

Updated November 8, 2026 · 8 min read

Short answer

NotebookLM (now called Gemini Notebook) costs $0 for personal use. The free plan gives you 100 notebooks with 50 sources each. Paid limits come with Google's AI subscriptions: Google AI Plus (100 sources per notebook), Google AI Pro at $19.99/month in the US (500 notebooks, 300 sources each) and Google AI Ultra (500+ sources). There is no separate NotebookLM subscription to buy.

Google changes packaging and prices often, so treat dollar amounts as pointers and check the plan page inside Gemini Notebook, or Google's AI plans page, before you pay. For a yes/no on the free plan, see is NotebookLM free.

Free plan: what most people use

Sign in at notebooklm.google.com with any personal Google account and start building notebooks. No credit card, no trial countdown.

You get up to 100 notebooks, 50 sources per notebook, cited chat across your sources, and every Studio output: Audio Overviews, Video Overviews, slide decks, mind maps, reports, flashcards and quizzes, within usage limits. That is enough for a full semester course, a client project or a personal research sprint. Size rules and workarounds are in our source limits guide.

Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra: when an upgrade makes sense

The old NotebookLM Plus and Google One AI Premium names are gone. Higher NotebookLM limits now come bundled with Google's AI subscriptions, which also include the Gemini app's top models, extra Google storage and other perks. You do not buy NotebookLM on its own.

  • Google AI Plus doubles sources to 100 per notebook and raises usage limits. Good if you only occasionally hit the 50-source wall.
  • Google AI Pro ($19.99/month in the US) gives 500 notebooks and 300 sources per notebook. This is the plan for big literature reviews or a whole degree in one notebook.
  • Google AI Ultra gives 500+ sources per notebook and the highest chat and Studio limits.

Upgrade if you need more than 50 sources in one notebook most weeks, or you hit the Audio Overview or chat limit regularly. Skip it if a few focused notebooks cover your work. Our 2026 review covers whether the free plan is enough for most readers.

Is there a student plan or discount?

Students use the same free plan as everyone else; a personal Google account is all you need. Google has also run free or discounted Google AI Pro offers for students in some countries, which lift NotebookLM to Pro limits. Check Google's student offer page for current eligibility in your country. More in our NotebookLM for students guide.

Workspace and enterprise pricing

Businesses and schools on Google Workspace get NotebookLM through their admin. Pricing is per user per month on Workspace Business, Enterprise and Education editions, and admins decide who can access it and whether higher limits apply. Workspace data is not used to train Google's models (see NotebookLM privacy). If you are a solo user, Workspace pricing does not matter to you.

How NotebookLM cost compares to other AI tools

NotebookLM is unusual because the research workflow (upload sources, get cited answers) is free. General assistants charge monthly:

  • ChatGPT Plus (~$20/month): broad chat, coding, writing. Less source-grounded than NotebookLM. Comparison
  • Claude Pro (~$20/month): strong reasoning and writing, Projects for documents.
  • Gemini app (free, or Google AI Pro for top models): the same subscription that raises your NotebookLM limits. Comparison
  • Perplexity Pro (~$20/month): web search and reports, different workflow.

Many people use NotebookLM free for document work and keep one paid assistant for everything else.

Which plan should you pick?

Pick Free if you are a student, solo researcher or writer. Follow our beginner tutorial and stay on free until you hit real limits.

Pick Google AI Plus if you sometimes need 51–100 sources in a notebook.

Pick Google AI Pro if you need up to 300 sources per notebook, or already want Gemini's top models and extra storage.

Pick Workspace only through your organization or school.

FAQ

How much does NotebookLM cost?

Nothing for personal use. NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook) is free with a Google account: 100 notebooks and 50 sources each. Higher limits come with Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro ($19.99/month in the US) or Google AI Ultra.

Is there still a NotebookLM Plus plan?

Not under that name. Google folded NotebookLM Plus into its Google AI subscriptions. Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra each raise NotebookLM's notebook, source and usage limits.

What does Google AI Pro add to NotebookLM?

500 notebooks instead of 100, 300 sources per notebook instead of 50, and higher chat and Studio usage limits. Each source can still be up to 500,000 words or 200 MB.

Do I need a subscription to use NotebookLM?

No. A free personal Google account is enough. There is no card and no trial.

Is there a NotebookLM student discount?

Students use the free plan. Google has also run free or discounted Google AI Pro offers for students in some countries; check Google's student page for current eligibility.
