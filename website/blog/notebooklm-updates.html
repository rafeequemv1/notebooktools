September 2026: rolling usage limits

Since September 2026, chat and Studio use (Audio Overviews, Video Overviews, slide decks, reports and the rest) is measured with limits that refresh every few hours, up to a weekly cap. Free accounts get a base allowance; Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra get more.

In practice:

  • If you hit a limit mid-session, wait a few hours and it refreshes.
  • Heavy weeks can reach the weekly cap; it resets later in the week.
  • Source limits did not change: they still depend on your plan, and each source can be up to 500,000 words or 200 MB.

If you see a limit message, our NotebookLM troubleshooting guide explains the options.

2026: NotebookLM becomes Gemini Notebook

Google renamed NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook) in 2026. It is the same product, existing notebooks carried over, and it is still at notebooklm.google.com. Read What is Gemini Notebook? for what the rename does and does not change, and NotebookLM vs Gemini for how it differs from the Gemini app.

Earlier feature timeline

Major features with dates confirmed by Google announcements or wide press coverage:

  1. March 2026: Google announced improvements to flashcards and quizzes, including saving progress, and added Cinematic Video Overviews.
  2. November 2025: Flashcards and quizzes arrived in the NotebookLM mobile app.
  3. September 8, 2025: Flashcards, quizzes and new report formats began rolling out on the web.
  4. July 29, 2025: Video Overviews launched (narrated slides, English first), along with a redesigned Studio panel that lets you create several outputs of the same type in one notebook.
  5. September 11, 2024: Audio Overviews launched, turning sources into a podcast-style discussion between two AI hosts.
