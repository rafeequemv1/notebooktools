Practical uses

10 ways to use it this week

01

Research a video topic in an hour

The job: You need to know what every other video on the topic already says.

  1. Import the top videos or a whole playlist in one click.
  2. Ask what they all cover and what nobody explains well.
  3. Turn the gaps into your outline.
YouTube importBulk import
02

Turn a slide deck into a blog post

The job: The talk is done; the article version never gets written.

  1. Open the notebook with your deck in Studio and click Blog in the ribbon.
  2. NotebookTools imports the deck, writes the post and embeds every slide as an image.
  3. Download it as HTML, Word, PDF or Markdown.
Slides to blog
03

Write how-to guides from screenshots

The job: Tutorials take hours because every step needs a screenshot.

  1. Do the task once and capture each step with Alt+Shift+S.
  2. Click Create how-to guide.
  3. Get a written, step-by-step guide with every screenshot in place.
Snapshots
04

Repurpose one source into many formats

The job: One great interview should become a podcast, a deck and a carousel.

  1. Add the source once.
  2. Generate an Audio Overview, slide deck, infographic and flashcards in Studio.
  3. Find every audio overview later in the Podcasts hub.
Studio & podcasts
05

Build a swipe file of posts that work

The job: Great hooks scroll past and are gone.

  1. Import standout posts from X, Reddit and LinkedIn as you see them.
  2. Tag them by format and topic in the notebooks table.
  3. Ask what the best-performing ones have in common.
X & social importNotebooks table
06

Turn your AI brainstorms into a script

The job: Your ideas are scattered across ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini chats.

  1. Import each conversation into one notebook.
  2. Ask for a single outline that merges the best ideas.
  3. Keep the original chats as sources to check against.
AI chat import
07

Write the weekly newsletter from your reading

The job: Friday comes and you can't remember what was worth sharing.

  1. Save highlights and pages to a newsletter notebook all week.
  2. Ask for the five strongest ideas, each with its source.
  3. Export the answer as Markdown for your newsletter tool.
HighlightsChat export
08

Run a content calendar in notebooks

The job: Every series and client needs its own research, kept apart.

  1. Make one notebook per series or client.
  2. Use folders for clients and tags for status (idea, drafting, published).
  3. Filter the table to see what's in progress.
Notebooks table
09

Keep a consistent visual style

The job: Every deck from AI looks different.

  1. Pick a slide style from the prompt library (editorial, minimal, bold).
  2. Insert it with / in the chat or copy it into Studio.
  3. Get decks that look like one brand.

10

Fact-check before you publish

The job: One wrong number can sink a post.

  1. Add your sources to the notebook behind the piece.
  2. Ask Gemini Notebook to verify each claim and show where it's from.
  3. Export the cited answer as your fact-check record.

Questions

Can Gemini Notebook write a blog post from my slides?

With NotebookTools, yes: the Blog workflow imports your slide deck, writes the post and embeds every slide as an image, ready to download.

Can I make a step-by-step tutorial from screenshots?

Yes. Capture each step with Snapshots (Alt+Shift+S), then click Create how-to guide to get a written guide with the screenshots in place.

Which AI chats can I import?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok and Kimi conversations, plus YouTube, X, Reddit, LinkedIn, Google Docs and any webpage.
