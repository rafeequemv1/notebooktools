# Text Splitter for NotebookLM

> NotebookLM accepts up to 500,000 words or 200 MB per source on every plan. If your text is longer, split it here: paste it or open a .txt or .md file, pick a part size, and the tool cuts it at paragraph breaks into numbered parts you can copy or download and add as separate sources.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/text-splitter
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. Paste your text, or open a .txt or .md file.
2. Give it a title so the parts are named, for example "Lecture transcripts (part 1 of 3)".
3. Pick a part size: the 500,000-word maximum, or smaller parts for sharper answers.
4. Copy or download each part and add it to NotebookLM as its own source.

## FAQ

### What is NotebookLM's limit per source?

Up to 500,000 words or 200 MB per source, on every plan, including the free one.

### Why split into smaller parts than the limit?

Smaller, focused sources often give more precise citations, and each part can be added or removed on its own. The trade-off is that each part uses one of your notebook's source slots.

### Does it cut sentences in half?

No. It splits at paragraph breaks, then at sentence ends if a single paragraph is too long.

### Is my file uploaded?

No. The file is read and split in your browser.
