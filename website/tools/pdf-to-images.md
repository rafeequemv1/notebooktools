# PDF Slides to Images

> Download your slide deck from NotebookLM as a PDF, drop it here, and every page becomes a PNG or JPG image: full resolution, or 1080 or 1200 px wide for LinkedIn and Instagram posts. Pick pages if you only need some, then download one image or all of them as a ZIP. Nothing is uploaded.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/pdf-to-images
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. In NotebookLM, open your slide deck in Studio and download it as a PDF.
2. Drop the PDF here.
3. Choose PNG or JPG, a size (1080 px for Instagram, 1200 px for LinkedIn) and, optionally, which pages.
4. Click Convert, then download single images or all of them as a ZIP.

## FAQ

### How do I download a NotebookLM slide deck?

Open the slide deck in the Studio panel and use its download option to save it as a PDF (or PowerPoint).

### Which size is best for LinkedIn and Instagram?

1080 px wide suits Instagram; 1200 px suits LinkedIn. For a swipeable LinkedIn carousel, post the PDF itself as a document.

### PNG or JPG?

PNG keeps text and lines crisp; JPG files are smaller, good for photo-heavy slides.

### Is my deck uploaded?

No. Pages are rendered in your browser with pdf.js.
