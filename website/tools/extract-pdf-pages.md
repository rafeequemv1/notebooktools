# Extract or Remove PDF Pages

> Drop a PDF to see every page as a thumbnail. Click pages (or type ranges like 12-40, 55) and choose Extract to save only those pages, or Remove to save the PDF without them. Giving NotebookLM only the chapters you need keeps its answers on topic and the source small.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/extract-pdf-pages
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. Drop the PDF; page thumbnails appear.
2. Click the pages you want, or type ranges such as 1-12, 20, 31-.
3. Choose Extract picked pages or Remove picked pages.
4. Save the new PDF and add it to NotebookLM.

## FAQ

### Why give NotebookLM only some pages?

Answers and summaries stay focused on what you're studying, citations are easier to check, and smaller sources leave room under the size limits.

### Can I type page numbers instead of clicking?

Yes. Use commas and ranges: 1-12, 20, 31- (31 to the end).

### Does it keep the text selectable?

Yes. Pages are copied as they are.

### Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
