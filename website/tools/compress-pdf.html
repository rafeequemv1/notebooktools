Compress PDF

Short answer

Drop a PDF and pick a mode. Keep text re-saves the file more compactly without changing anything you can see (small savings, text stays selectable). Scanned redraws each page as a JPEG at the quality you choose, which shrinks scans and photo-heavy PDFs a lot, but turns text into an image. Use it to get files under NotebookLM's 200 MB per source.

How to use it

  1. Drop the PDF.
  2. Choose Keep text for normal documents, or Scanned / image-heavy for scans and photo PDFs (pick a quality).
  3. Click Compress and compare the before and after size.
  4. Download the smaller PDF and add it to NotebookLM.

FAQ

Why won't my PDF upload to NotebookLM?

The most common reasons are size (over 200 MB per source), a password, or a damaged file. Compress or split large files; remove the password before uploading.

Will compressing hurt NotebookLM's answers?

Keep text mode changes nothing NotebookLM reads. Scanned mode turns pages into images, so NotebookLM has to read them like a scan; use it for files that are scans already.

How much smaller will it get?

Scanned PDFs often shrink by half or more at Balanced quality. Text-only PDFs are usually compact already, so Keep text saves less.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser with pdf.js and pdf-lib.
