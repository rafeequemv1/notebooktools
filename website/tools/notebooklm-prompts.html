How to use it

  1. Choose where the prompt goes with the tabs (Chat, Audio Overview, Slide deck…) and, if you like, a job such as Study or Research.
  2. Click a prompt card.
  3. Type into the highlighted blanks inside the prompt, or click "Use examples".
  4. For a slide deck, pick a visual style (optional).
  5. Copy the prompt and follow the "Paste it here" steps for that destination.

FAQ

What makes a good NotebookLM prompt?

Name the output, the audience, the length and the focus, and tell NotebookLM to stay within your sources. Specific formats (a table, 10 questions, 8 slides) work better than open questions.

Where do I paste a prompt in NotebookLM?

Chat prompts go in the chat box at the bottom of the notebook. Studio prompts go in the Customise box of that output: click the pencil on Audio Overview, Video Overview, Slide deck, Flashcards, Quiz or Infographic, or choose Reports, then Create your own.

Do these prompts work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM; the chat and Studio work the same way.

Can I use these prompts inside NotebookLM without copying?

Yes, with the free NotebookTools Chrome extension: type / in the NotebookLM chat for your prompts, and slide styles appear as cards in the slide deck dialog (Pro).
