# Merge PDFs

> Drop two or more PDFs (or JPG and PNG images), put them in order with the arrows, and click Merge to download one combined PDF. In NotebookLM every file is one source and the free plan allows 50 per notebook, so merging related handouts or chapters saves slots. Everything happens in your browser.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/merge-pdf
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. Drop the PDFs (and any JPG or PNG images) you want to combine.
2. Use the arrows to put them in order; remove any with ×.
3. Click Merge and download.
4. Add the merged PDF to NotebookLM as one source.

## FAQ

### Should I merge PDFs before adding them to NotebookLM?

Merge files that belong together (one course's handouts, one report's appendices) to save source slots. Keep unrelated papers separate: citations then point to the right document.

### Is there a size limit?

Your browser's memory is the only limit here. NotebookLM accepts up to 200 MB or 500,000 words per source, so check the merged file with the page & word counter.

### Can I merge images too?

Yes. JPG and PNG images become full-size pages in the merged PDF.

### Are my files uploaded?

No. Merging happens in your browser with pdf-lib.
