# PDF to Text / Markdown

> Drop a PDF to pull out its text, with lines that were wrapped mid-sentence joined back up. Choose plain text or Markdown (headings and bullet lists detected), optionally mark where each page starts, and turn on OCR to read scanned pages that have no text layer. Copy the result or download a .txt or .md file to add to NotebookLM as a clean text source.

## How to use it

1. Drop a PDF in the box.
2. Choose Markdown or plain text. Keep "Join broken line wraps" on for readable paragraphs.
3. If some pages are scanned, turn on OCR scanned pages.
4. Copy the text or download it, then add it to NotebookLM (Copied text, or upload the .md/.txt file).

## FAQ

### Why convert a PDF to text before adding it to NotebookLM?

NotebookLM reads PDFs directly, but multi-column layouts, headers and footers and scans can confuse it. A clean text or Markdown version often gives more accurate answers and citations.

### Can it read scanned PDFs?

Yes. Turn on OCR scanned pages: pages without a text layer are read with Tesseract OCR in your browser (English). Check names and numbers afterwards.

### Does it keep tables and formatting?

It keeps paragraphs, and detects headings and bullet lists for Markdown. Complex tables come out as text in reading order.

### Is my PDF uploaded?

No. pdf.js and Tesseract run in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
