# Free tools for NotebookLM

> Thirteen free tools for people who work in NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook), all running in your browser with nothing uploaded. A slide editor turns NotebookLM slide decks into editable PowerPoint files. PDF and image tools get files ready for NotebookLM: split, merge, compress, extract pages, count words against the limits, convert PDF to text, read images with OCR, and turn slide decks into images. NotebookLM tools help inside it: a prompt library, a link cleaner, a text splitter and an answer cleaner.

Updated: 2026-11-22

## Tools

- [NotebookLM Prompt Library](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts): 27 fill-in-the-blank prompts for chat, Audio and Video Overviews, slide decks, reports, flashcards, quizzes and infographics.
- [Link List Cleaner](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/link-list-cleaner): Paste notes, a reading list or HTML. Get a clean list of unique links, ready to add to NotebookLM.
- [Text Splitter for NotebookLM](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/text-splitter): Too long for one source? Split a transcript or book into parts that fit NotebookLM's 500,000-word limit.
- [NotebookLM Answer Cleaner](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-answer-cleaner): Copied an answer from NotebookLM? Strip the citation numbers and … markers. Get clean text or Markdown.
- [NotebookLM Slide Editor (PDF to PPTX)](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/edit-notebooklm-slides): Make a NotebookLM slide deck editable: OCR turns the text into real text boxes. Edit, present, download PowerPoint.
- [Split PDF for NotebookLM](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/split-pdf-for-notebooklm): Book or PDF too big for one source? Split it into equal parts, every N pages, by ranges or under a size limit.
- [Merge PDFs](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/merge-pdf): Combine lecture handouts, papers or scans (and images) into one PDF. One source instead of ten.
- [PDF to Text / Markdown](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/pdf-to-text): Get clean text or Markdown from any PDF, with OCR for scanned pages. Fixes broken line wraps.
- [PDF Page & Word Counter](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/pdf-page-word-counter): Will it fit in NotebookLM? Count pages, words and size for one PDF or a whole folder, against the limits.
- [Compress PDF](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/compress-pdf): Shrink large or scanned PDFs so they upload. Keep text, or compress scans hard with a quality slider.
- [Extract or Remove PDF Pages](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/extract-pdf-pages): Keep only the chapters you need, or delete pages. Click thumbnails or type ranges like 12-40, 55.
- [Image to Text (OCR)](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/image-to-text-ocr): Photos of whiteboards, slides, handwritten notes or screenshots to editable text. Paste or drop.
- [PDF Slides to Images](https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/pdf-to-images): Turn a NotebookLM slide deck (or any PDF) into PNG or JPG images sized for LinkedIn and Instagram.
