Split PDF for NotebookLM

Short answer

NotebookLM accepts up to 200 MB or 500,000 words per source, so large textbooks and scanned books often fail to upload. Drop the PDF here and split it into equal parts, every N pages, by page ranges (one part per line) or into parts under a size you choose. Each part downloads as its own PDF, or all at once as a ZIP, ready to add as separate sources. The file never leaves your browser.

How to use it

  1. Drop your PDF in the box, or click Choose a file.
  2. Pick how to split: into equal parts, every N pages, by page ranges, or under a size limit (190 MB keeps each part under NotebookLM's 200 MB cap).
  3. Click Split PDF.
  4. Download each part, or all of them as a ZIP, and add each part to your notebook as its own source.

FAQ

What is the maximum PDF size for NotebookLM?

Each source can be up to 200 MB or 500,000 words, on every plan. Split anything larger into parts.

How many parts should I split a book into?

Split by chapters if you can (By page ranges), so each source stays on one topic and citations are easier to follow. Otherwise, equal parts under 200 MB work.

Does splitting lose quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, with their text, images and fonts; nothing is re-compressed.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is read and split in your browser with pdf-lib. Nothing is sent to a server.
