# Image to Text (OCR)

> Drop or paste photos of whiteboards, lecture slides, book pages, receipts or screenshots, and the text is read out with OCR (Tesseract, English) right in your browser. Several images become one document with a heading per image. Copy it or download a Markdown file to add to NotebookLM as a text source.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/image-to-text-ocr
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. Drop one or more images, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac).
2. Wait while each image is read. The first run loads the text reader (about 8 MB, then cached).
3. Fix anything misread directly in the text box.
4. Copy the text or download it, then add it to NotebookLM as a source.

## FAQ

### Does it read handwriting?

Neat print works best. Joined-up handwriting is often misread; check the text before you rely on it.

### Which languages does it support?

English. Other Latin-script text is often readable but less accurate.

### NotebookLM accepts images; why convert them?

Text sources are searchable, quotable and easy to correct. For notes and whiteboards, clean text usually gives NotebookLM more reliable answers.

### Are my images uploaded?

No. OCR runs in your browser with Tesseract.js; images never leave your device.
