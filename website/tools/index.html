FAQ

Are these NotebookLM tools free?

Yes. All thirteen are free with no sign-up, and they run in your browser, so nothing you paste or open is uploaded.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF tools use pdf-lib, pdf.js and Tesseract.js running in your browser tab. Files never leave your device, which also makes them fast for large files.

Are these tools made by Google?

No. NotebookTools is independent and not affiliated with Google. The tools work with NotebookLM (now Gemini Notebook) as it is today.

What is the NotebookTools extension?

A Chrome extension for NotebookLM: one-click imports from YouTube, web pages, X and AI chats are free; Pro ($49 once) adds a notebooks dashboard, source manager, slide style cards, chat export and more.
