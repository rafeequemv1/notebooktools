# PDF Page & Word Counter

> Drop one PDF or a whole batch. The counter reads every page in your browser and shows pages, words and size per file, then checks them against NotebookLM's limits: 500,000 words or 200 MB per source, and 50 sources per notebook on the free plan. It also flags files that look scanned.

- Tool: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/pdf-page-word-counter
- Price: free, no sign-up
- Privacy: runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## How to use it

1. Drop one or more PDFs.
2. Wait while each file's pages are read and counted.
3. Check the meters: words and size of the largest file, and the number of files against 50 sources.
4. Follow the suggestion: split files that are too big, or merge or spread files across notebooks.

## FAQ

### How many words can a NotebookLM source have?

Up to 500,000 words, and up to 200 MB, per source on every plan.

### How many PDFs can I add to one notebook?

50 sources on the free plan, 100 on Google AI Plus, 300 on Google AI Pro, and more on Ultra. Each PDF is one source.

### Why does a PDF show 0 words?

It's probably scanned: the pages are images with no text layer. NotebookLM can still read many scans, but converting with OCR first gives more reliable answers.

### Are my files uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser with pdf.js.
