# Key numbers at a glance: NotebookLM Infographic prompt

> The most important figures from your sources in one shareable graphic. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Infographic).

## The prompt

```
Make an infographic with the [How many numbers] most important numbers about [Topic] from the sources. Each number large, with a one-line explanation and the source name in small text. Group related numbers together and add a one-line headline that states the overall message.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open Studio on the right.
2. Click the pencil on Infographic (Customise).
3. Paste the prompt into the description box and generate.

## When to use it

Reports, posts or slides that need the numbers to land quickly.

**Tip.** Check every number against its citation before you share the image.

## Example, filled in

```
Make an infographic with the 6 most important numbers about global renewable energy in 2025 from the sources. Each number large, with a one-line explanation and the source name in small text. Group related numbers together and add a one-line headline that states the overall message.
```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM infographic prompt?

Open Studio on the right. Click the pencil on Infographic (Customise). Paste the prompt into the description box and generate.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
