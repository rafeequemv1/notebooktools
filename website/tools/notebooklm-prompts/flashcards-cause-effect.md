## The prompt

```
Make flashcards on causes and effects in [Topic]. Front: "What caused X?" or "What happened because of Y?". Back: the answer in one or two sentences, naming the mechanism, not just the event.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open Studio on the right.
2. Click the pencil on Flashcards (Customise).
3. Paste the prompt into the topic box and generate.

## When to use it

History, economics, biology and any subject where understanding causes matters.

**Tip.** Mix these with definition cards for a complete set.

## Example, filled in

```
Make flashcards on causes and effects in the French Revolution. Front: "What caused X?" or "What happened because of Y?". Back: the answer in one or two sentences, naming the mechanism, not just the event.
```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM flashcards prompt?

Open Studio on the right. Click the pencil on Flashcards (Customise). Paste the prompt into the topic box and generate.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
