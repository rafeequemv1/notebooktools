# Explain it like a tutor: NotebookLM Chat prompt

> A patient, step-by-step explanation of one hard concept, checked against your sources. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Chat).

- Page: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts/explain-like-a-tutor
- Fill the blanks interactively: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts#explain-like-a-tutor
- Jobs: Study, Teach
- Updated: 2026-11-21

## The prompt

```
Explain [Concept] to me as [Your level], the way a good tutor would.

Start with a one-sentence plain-English answer. Then build it up in three short steps, each with an everyday example. Point out the part most people get wrong. Finish with two quick questions I can answer to check I understood, and put the answers at the very end.

Stay within my sources and cite them.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open your notebook.
2. Tick the sources the prompt should use.
3. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

## When to use it

When a chapter or lecture lost you and you want one concept explained from the ground up.

**Tip.** Ask a follow-up such as "go deeper on step 2" instead of starting a new chat, so it keeps the context.

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM chat prompt?

Open your notebook. Tick the sources the prompt should use. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
