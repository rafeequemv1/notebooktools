Where to paste it

When to use it

Teachers planning a class from a textbook chapter, articles or videos in the notebook.

Tip. Add your school's curriculum standard as a source to have the objectives match it.

Example, filled in

What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.
