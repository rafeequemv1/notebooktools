# Devil's advocate review: NotebookLM Chat prompt

> Finds the weakest claims in your draft and what the sources say against them. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Chat).

- Page: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts/devils-advocate
- Fill the blanks interactively: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts#devils-advocate
- Jobs: Research, Write
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## The prompt

```
Act as a sceptical reviewer of "[Name of your draft source]". Using the other sources in this notebook:

1. List the 5 weakest claims in it, quoting each one.
2. For each, say why it is weak and what the other sources say (with citations).
3. Suggest how to fix or soften each claim.

Be direct. Do not praise the draft.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open your notebook.
2. Tick the sources the prompt should use.
3. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

## When to use it

When your own draft or proposal is a source and you want it stress-tested before others see it.

**Tip.** Upload the draft as a Google Doc so you can refresh it after edits.

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM chat prompt?

Open your notebook. Tick the sources the prompt should use. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
