# Argued essay outline: NotebookLM Chat prompt

> A thesis and section-by-section outline with the evidence for each point. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Chat).

- Page: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts/essay-outline
- Fill the blanks interactively: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts#essay-outline
- Jobs: Write, Study
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## The prompt

```
Help me plan a [Word count]-word essay answering: [Essay question]

Propose a one-sentence thesis. Then outline 4 to 5 sections: for each, the point it makes, the evidence from my sources (with citations) and one counter-argument to address. Suggest roughly how many words each section should get. Do not write the essay itself.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open your notebook.
2. Tick the sources the prompt should use.
3. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

## When to use it

Before writing an essay or article from your research, to plan the argument.

**Tip.** Ask for a second thesis that argues the opposite side to test your argument.

## Example, filled in

```
Help me plan a 2,000-word essay answering: Was the printing press the main cause of the Reformation?

```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM chat prompt?

Open your notebook. Tick the sources the prompt should use. Paste the prompt into the chat box at the bottom and send.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
