# Case study walkthrough: NotebookLM Video Overview prompt

> Walks through one case: the situation, what was done and what happened. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Video Overview).

## The prompt

```
Walk through [Case] as a case study. Structure: the situation before, what went wrong or what decision was made, what happened next (with numbers from the sources), and three lessons. Use a timeline visual for the sequence of events.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open Studio on the right.
2. Click the pencil on Video Overview (Customise).
3. Paste the prompt into the focus box and generate.

## When to use it

Training, pitches or classes built around a real example in your sources.

**Tip.** Add a source with figures (a report or data table) for stronger numbers.

## Example, filled in

```
Walk through the 2021 Suez Canal blockage as a case study. Structure: the situation before, what went wrong or what decision was made, what happened next (with numbers from the sources), and three lessons. Use a timeline visual for the sequence of events.
```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM video overview prompt?

Open Studio on the right. Click the pencil on Video Overview (Customise). Paste the prompt into the focus box and generate.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
