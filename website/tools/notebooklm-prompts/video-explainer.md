# Visual explainer: NotebookLM Video Overview prompt

> A Video Overview that explains one idea step by step with simple visuals. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Video Overview).

- Page: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts/video-explainer
- Fill the blanks interactively: https://www.notebooktools.com/tools/notebooklm-prompts#video-explainer
- Jobs: Teach, Summarize
- Updated: 2026-11-22

## The prompt

```
Explain [Topic] for [Audience]. Show it as a sequence of clear steps, one idea per slide, with a simple diagram or labelled image for each step. Keep text on screen to a few words. Finish with a one-slide recap of all the steps.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open Studio on the right.
2. Click the pencil on Video Overview (Customise).
3. Paste the prompt into the focus box and generate.

## When to use it

Explaining a process or system that is easier to see than to read.

**Tip.** If the result is too dense, ask for fewer steps and regenerate.

## Example, filled in

```
Explain how a bill becomes law for high-school students. Show it as a sequence of clear steps, one idea per slide, with a simple diagram or labelled image for each step. Keep text on screen to a few words. Finish with a one-slide recap of all the steps.
```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM video overview prompt?

Open Studio on the right. Click the pencil on Video Overview (Customise). Paste the prompt into the focus box and generate.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
