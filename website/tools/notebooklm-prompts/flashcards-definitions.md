# Definitions and terms: NotebookLM Flashcards prompt

> Flashcards for the vocabulary of a course: term on the front, meaning on the back. Replace the [blanks], then paste it in NotebookLM (Flashcards).

## The prompt

```
Make flashcards for the key terms in [Topic]. Front: the term. Back: a definition in under 20 words and one short example. Skip terms that are not defined or explained in the sources.
```

## Where to paste it

1. Open Studio on the right.
2. Click the pencil on Flashcards (Customise).
3. Paste the prompt into the topic box and generate.

## When to use it

Early in a course, or for subjects with a lot of vocabulary (law, biology, languages).

**Tip.** Review the cards a few days apart; spaced repetition beats one long session.

## Example, filled in

```
Make flashcards for the key terms in contract law. Front: the term. Back: a definition in under 20 words and one short example. Skip terms that are not defined or explained in the sources.
```

## FAQ

### Where do I paste a NotebookLM flashcards prompt?

Open Studio on the right. Click the pencil on Flashcards (Customise). Paste the prompt into the topic box and generate.

### What do the [brackets] mean?

They are blanks. Replace each one with your own words before you paste the prompt, or fill them in the prompt library, which shows them as fields inside the text.

### Does this prompt work in Gemini Notebook?

Yes. Gemini Notebook is the new name for NotebookLM, and the chat and Studio work the same way.
