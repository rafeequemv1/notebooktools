FAQ

Why does a copied NotebookLM answer have numbers in it?

Those are the citation chips that link each claim to a source passage. When copied as text they turn into bare numbers and "…" markers.

Will it remove numbers that are part of my content?

It only removes short numbers that sit right before punctuation or a line end, the way citation chips do, plus [1]-style references. Years and numbers inside sentences are kept.

Can I keep the citations instead?

Turn off "Remove citation numbers". To export answers with formatting and sources intact, use NotebookTools chat export.

Is the answer uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
