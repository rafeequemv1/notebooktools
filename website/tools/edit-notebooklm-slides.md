## How to use it

1. In NotebookLM, open your slide deck in Studio and download it as a PDF.
2. Drop the PDF here (or click Try with a sample file). Keep "Make the text editable" on.
3. Wait a few seconds per slide while the text is read. Slides that are ready can be edited straight away.
4. Double-click any text to change it. Drag to move, use the handles to resize, and use the panel on the right for font, size, colour and alignment.
5. Add text, shapes or images from the toolbar, compare with the original, or press Present.
6. Click Download PowerPoint for an editable .pptx (or choose PDF or PNG from the arrow menu).

## FAQ

### Can you edit NotebookLM slide decks?

Not inside NotebookLM: its slide decks are generated as images, so the text can't be changed. This editor reads the text with OCR and turns it into real text boxes you can edit.

### How do I convert NotebookLM slides to PowerPoint?

Download the slide deck as a PDF from NotebookLM's Studio panel, open it here and click Download PowerPoint. Each slide keeps its design as the background, and the text comes back as editable text boxes.

### Will it look exactly like the original?

Close, not identical. Fonts are matched to the nearest common font, and the area behind the old text is filled from the surrounding colours: clean on flat or gradient backgrounds, but busy photos can show faint marks. Use Original to compare, and fix anything in the editor.

### What about charts and diagrams?

Text inside charts, diagrams and pictures is usually left as part of the image so the graphic stays intact. You can add your own text boxes on top.

### Does it work in Google Slides?

Yes. In Google Slides choose File → Import slides (or open the .pptx from Drive); the text boxes stay editable.

### Is my deck uploaded?

No. pdf.js, Tesseract OCR, pdf-lib and PptxGenJS all run in your browser. The deck never leaves your device.
