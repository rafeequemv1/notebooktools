## How to use it

1. Choose the output: slide deck, study guide, quiz, Audio Overview, briefing or flashcards.
2. Set the audience and length, and type an optional focus.
3. For a slide deck, click a style card to add its visual direction.
4. Copy the prompt and paste it where the note says (for slides: Studio → Slide deck → Customise).

## FAQ

### What makes a good NotebookLM prompt?

Name the output, the audience, the length and the focus, and tell NotebookLM to use only your sources. Specific formats (a table, 10 questions, 8 slides) work better than open questions.

### Where do I paste a slide deck prompt in NotebookLM?

In Studio, open Slide deck, click the pencil to customise, and paste the prompt into the box that describes the deck you want.

### Are the slide styles free?

Yes, on this page. Inside NotebookLM, the NotebookTools extension shows the same styles as one-click cards in the slide deck dialog (a Pro feature).

### Does this tool send my text anywhere?

No. The prompt is built in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
