How to use it

  1. Paste text or HTML that contains links.
  2. Keep tracking removal on, and choose all links, YouTube only or web pages only.
  3. Check the count against NotebookLM's 50-sources-per-notebook limit.
  4. Copy the list or download it, then add the links to your notebook.

FAQ

How many links can I add to one NotebookLM notebook?

Each link becomes one source. The free plan allows 50 sources per notebook; Google AI Plus allows 100, Google AI Pro 300 and Ultra 500+.

Does it remove duplicate links?

Yes. Links that point to the same page are kept once, including YouTube short links (youtu.be) and full watch links for the same video.

What tracking does it remove?

Common tracking parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, fbclid, gclid and similar. The rest of the link is kept.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser; nothing leaves your device.
