Thanks for installing NotebookTools

Import YouTube videos, webpages, X posts, AI chats and highlighted text into NotebookLM.

Get set up in three steps.

Pin the extension

Keep NotebookTools one click away in your toolbar.

  1. Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Find NotebookTools and click the pin next to it.

Pinned. Click the NotebookTools icon any time to open the side panel.

Open NotebookLM and sign in

NotebookTools adds sources with your NotebookLM account, so keep it signed in in this Chrome profile.

Open NotebookLM

Done. Stay signed in and your notebooks show up in the side panel.

Try your first import

On a YouTube watch page, click NotebookLM next to Like and Share. On any other page, click the NotebookTools icon to open the side panel and add the current tab.

Open YouTube video

Your first source is in NotebookLM. This step ticked itself.

More ways to import

  • Select text on any page, right-click, and choose Add to NotebookLM.
  • On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, X, Reddit and more, use the NotebookLM button on the page.
  • Open the side panel's Bulk tab to import many links, open tabs or a playlist at once.
